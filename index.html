A little light on the subject

Your just-right light starts here.

Read the light guide →
01

Start with what you do.

Reading, hosting, or simply slowing down? Choose a focused task light for getting things done, or a shaded table lamp for a softer, all-around glow.

Our tip: layer two or three light sources
02

Give it a little room.

Let the lamp fit the furniture, not fight it. For a reading corner, keep the bottom of the shade close to seated eye level so the bulb stays out of sight.

Our tip: measure your spot before you shop
03

Warm is a feeling.

Look for 2200–2700K for that golden, end-of-day warmth. Add a dimmer and let the same lamp take you from one more task to one more chapter.

Our tip: lower kelvins = a warmer glow
